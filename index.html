How it works

Four simple steps

Hindi mein bhi likh sakte hain. Your story matters more than your grammar.

Step 01
Pick your category
Leader, Team, or Chill Cockroach. Choose what fits. All three are equally welcome in the colony.
Step 02
Share your story
Tell us what you've done or who you are — in Hindi or English. Attach links to reels, RTI filings, reports, or anything that shows your work.
Step 03
Get shortlisted
A panel of journalists, activists, and citizens shortlists nominees in each category. Shortlisted entries are published publicly.
Step 04
Awards ceremony
Winners announced at a public event — live-streamed and open to all. Every nominee gets recognition. Every story gets heard.
